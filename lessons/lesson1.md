## What is programming?
Comparing what a computer and a human would do, there is this senario where you understand how different a machine is from a human, imagine if you ask a human to brush his or her teeth, they would know exactly what to do and how to do it, but a machine can only do what you explicitly state down and explain for it to do in the manner that you have laid it out for it. That is what makes the difference between a normal human and a robot or a computer. A computer needs sequencing and precision for it to do what it is supposed to do.
*** Programming *** is simply the skill of talking to the computer or a machine that does exactly what you say, in the exact order you say it, without ever guessing what you mean. for the example given about the brushing of teeth, you will understand that there are three ideas behind every computer program ever written: 
- Sequencing 
- Precision
- State
### key concepts
#### Programming
This is the act of giving a computer a list of instructions to reach a goal. Just like writing down the steps of how to brush one's teeth for someone who has never done it before.
#### Sequencing
This is defined as the exact order the instructions given to the computer run in. Change the order and the outcome changes. In programming, the computer executes instructions from top to bottom, one line at a time. This is refered to as *** execution order.*** so there is every chance of making the computer go wrong not because the instructions are not correct but simply because they dont have the right order.
#### Precision
This is simply eliminating all room for interpretation. There is a clear difference between telling the robot to squeeze out the tootpaste on the toothbrush and telling it to squeeze out 2-centimeter strip of toothpaste onto the brush. With precision, there is zero guess work.
##### precision vs vague instructions.
- vague instructions are bad, because they dont carry or convey with them the right and direct or exact thing the computer needs to do thereby giving room for the computer to guess and make errors.
- precise instructions are those that convey with them the exactness a computer needs to execute an instruction and make sure everything is in order. 
#### State
This is simply the current status of something at a given moment. As the computer executes instructions, it follows steps to do that and each step is known as a state. State here is seen to be a whiteboard the computer uses to track the current status of things. If the state of a program is not properly tracked, the program will make wrong decisions.
By keeping track of state, the computer would always know where it is and what it is supposed to do next.

In the light of the above description of what programming is, *** Programming *** is the art of breaking a task down so clearly that even a brainless robot can do it perfectly, every single time. 
### Common mistakes and self-checking clarifications
- Assuming the machine knows what it is supposed to do or what you mean. the computer doesn't know what you want it to do, unless of course you detail everythin you need it to do for you.
- Updating state in the wring order. Always make sure to use the right order for the computer to follow while executing your instructions.
- vague measurements. Using words like "quickly", "slowly", etc will not make the computer understand what you mean or want, the computer understands actual measurements like unit of numbers, times and exact true or false stats.
